The Crisis

Africa's Water Loss
Crisis Is Silent

Across Sub-Saharan Africa, water utilities are producing safe, treated water and watching it vanish — into broken pipes, undetected leaks, and unchecked infrastructure. The losses are invisible. The consequences are not.

ACTIVE CRISIS — Rwanda is losing water right now. The infrastructure exists. The detection does not.
43%
Non-Revenue Water Rate
Nearly half of all water Rwanda produces is lost before it is billed — through leaks, burst pipes, and undetected distribution failures.
RWF 2.1B
Annual Revenue Lost
Rwanda's national water utility WASAC loses over RWF 2.1 billion every year to water that leaves the network and is never recovered.
72 hrs
Average Detection Time
Today, a burst pipe is identified when a resident calls to complain. By then, millions of litres are gone. Manual methods cannot solve this.
27%
Households Affected
Over a quarter of urban Rwandan households experience unreliable water supply — a direct consequence of distribution losses going undetected.

Sources: WASAC Annual Report 2023 · MININFRA Infrastructure Development Report 2022 · World Bank WASH Data 2023 · UN-Habitat Urban Water Report 2022

Our Approach

We Find the Loss.
Instantly.

HydroSense monitors every checkpoint in a city's water distribution network — from the treatment plant all the way to the household meter. We compare what enters each segment against what leaves it, continuously, in real time.

When the numbers don't match, we know immediately. We know which segment is losing water, how much is being lost, and the rate at which it is flowing out. That information reaches the utility's operations team in under 500 milliseconds.

No waiting for a call. No sending engineers to walk pipes. No guessing. The exact location — identified, flagged, ready for repair.

📍

Precise Segment Identification

We pinpoint the exact section of pipe where water is being lost — not just the general area.

⚡

500ms Alert Latency

From leak to alert in half a second. Not 72 hours. Repair crews are dispatched while the leak is fresh.

🌍

Built for African Infrastructure

Designed specifically for the distribution networks found across Sub-Saharan African cities — not adapted from European systems.

Rwanda's 43% → 20% NRW target becomes achievable

Rwanda's Ministry of Infrastructure (MININFRA) has committed to reducing Non-Revenue Water from 43% to 20% by 2030 as a core pillar of Vision 2050. That goal cannot be achieved with manual inspection methods that rely on resident complaints and physical pipe walks. It requires real-time data, automated detection, and instant alerting. HydroSense is the technology that makes that target achievable — and Rwanda is our proof point for the entire continent.

Our Story

Built by Africans.
For Africa.

HydroSense was born at the African Leadership University in Kigali — where a student from Nigeria and a student from Rwanda looked at the same water crisis from two different sides of the continent and decided to build the solution. We did not read about this problem in a report. We lived it.

🇳🇬
SI
Success Ituma
Co-Founder
Software Engineering Student · African Leadership University, Kigali

Success grew up in Obuchiru Ezillo, a deeply underserved rural community in Ebonyi State, Nigeria — where pipe-borne water does not exist. His community relied on streams and boreholes. He taught himself to code, earned a Mastercard Foundation Scholarship, and came to ALU Rwanda. When he discovered that Rwanda was losing nearly half its treated water to undetected leaks, he could not look away. That is why HydroSense exists.
